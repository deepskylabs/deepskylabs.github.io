Privacy Policy for DiGiCALc

Last updated: October 28, 2026

DeepSky Labs developed the DiGiCALc calculator application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use DiGiCALc.

1. Information We Collect

DiGiCALc does not collect, store, or transmit personal information from users.

The application does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, address, or email address.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

DiGiCALc may store application preferences, settings, or calculation history locally on your device.

Information stored locally by the application remains on your device and is not transmitted to DeepSky Labs.

3. Internet Access

The core functionality of DiGiCALc does not require an internet connection.

4. Third-Party Services

DiGiCALc does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Children's Privacy

DiGiCALc does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Since DiGiCALc does not collect or transmit personal information, DeepSky Labs does not maintain users' personal information on its servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact DeepSky Labs.
